Each self solves for its own felt experience and its forecast of the rest of its life, given what it knows. Forecasts expect no further news. Every channel is linear, and every kernel geometric. Memory is afterglow minus adaptation, the biphasic extension; at c = 0 it is the canonical model's afterglow alone.
